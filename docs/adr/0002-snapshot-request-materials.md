# Snapshot preparation request details

Lab exercises provide reusable material requirements, and groups provide recurring slots, but requests and printed tickets must preserve what staff were asked to prepare at the time and where. Each request will retain its material list, calculated quantities, lab, and session time, including the professor's permitted additions and omissions. Later edits to the exercise or group do not silently change submitted requests; a group schedule change flags affected future requests for staff confirmation. Editing a reviewed request returns it to open and requires a new ticket.

The schedule-change and reviewed-request edit paths are deferred for the first preparation-request release. [ADR 0008](0008-keep-initial-preparation-requests-simple.md) records the initial constraints.
